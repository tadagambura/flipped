# Flipped

**Know your real profit from reselling**

> #17 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Several times a week · Medium · Revenue potential: Medium · Money & Work

## What it is

For Vinted, eBay and Depop sellers. Log what you paid, the fees, postage and what it sold for, and Flipped shows your real profit per item and per month, what's still unsold, and how close you are to the levels where platforms report sellers to HMRC.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Reselling is a huge UK side hustle, and since 2024 platforms have reported sellers above set thresholds to HMRC, which gives sellers a new reason to keep records. Logging every sale means frequent, natural use.

## Who it's for

Casual and side-hustle resellers on Vinted, eBay, Depop and at car boot sales.

**The problem:** Sellers don't know their real profit after fees and postage, and aren't sure about tax reporting.

## Version one

- Log items: cost, platform, fees, postage, sale price
- Profit per item and per month
- Unsold stock with days listed
- Reporting-threshold tracker (sales count and total; current rules to verify)
- Monthly recap

**Example:** A seller logs 40 Vinted sales in a month, sees £310 real profit after postage, and spots five items that have sat unsold for 60 days.
